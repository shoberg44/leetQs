Q2: Delayed Gratitude

You have just logged into your favorite MMORPG, CloudLattice online! You are greeted by your trusty NPC (non-player character) friend, Sierra, who has been your faithful companion in the world of CloudLattice for many months. As always, she greets you by giving you some CloudCoinsTM.

From experience, you know this is part of some weird social experiment (presumably being run by the game developers). On every second that you wait before thanking Sierra for the gift, the number of CloudCoinsTM you are receiving will change according to the following rules:

If your have an odd number of CloudCoinsTM, you lose 15 CloudCoinsTM and the remaining amount of CloudCoinsTM is doubled.

If your have an even number of CloudCoinsTM, you lose 99 CloudCoinsTM but the amount you have remaining is tripled.

If you have more than 1,000,000 CloudCoinsTM your total loops back around (so 1,000,007 CloudCoinsTM  becomes just 7 CloudCoinsTM). Likewise, if your total goes below 0 CloudCoinsTM it ALSO loops around (so -7 CloudCoinsTM would become 999,993 CloudCoinsTM).

Given a starting number of CloudCoinsTM ( S ) and a number of seconds you could wait ( k ), determine how many CloudCoinsTM you would earn.

Input Format The first line is a number ( T ), indicating the number of Test cases. The next T lines each have two values, S and k, indicating the starting amount and the waiting time, respectively.

Constraints

1 <= T <= 100
0 <= S <= 1,000,000
0 <= k <= 100
Output Format: T lines, each indicating the amount of CloudCoinsTM you would get

Example 0
Sample Input

3
1000 1
1000 2
1000 5
Sample Output

2703
5376
94599
Explanation Three test cases are provided. Each starts with 1,000 CloudCoinsTM.

In the first case, you just wait one second. Since you are given an even number of CloudCoinsTM, 99 are subtracted (leaving 901) and this result is tripled, giving you  2703 CloudCoinsTM.

In the second test, you wait two seconds. The first second results in 2703 CloudCoinsTM (as above), but the total is now odd; as such, waiting another second subtracts 15 CloudCoinsTM (brining the total to 2688) and then doubles the total to 5376 CloudCoinsTM.

In the third test case, you wait five seconds. Since the value after two seconds is even, 99 CloudCoinsTM are subtracted (putting your total at 5277) and the value is tripled to 15,831. Next 15 CloudCoinsTM are subtracted (producing 15,816) and the total is doubled (31,632). Finally 99 CloudCoinsTM are subtracted again (31,533) and the total is tripled to 94,599.

1 is odd, so we subtract 15 CloudCoinsTM, which results in a total of -14, which loops around to 999,986. We double that, giving us 1,999,972, which loops back to 999,972 CloudCoinsTM.

999,972 is even, so we subtract 99 (getting 999,873) and triple it (getting 2,999,619, looping back to 999,619).

999,619 is odd, so we subtract 15 (999,604) and double it (1,999,208 looping to 999,208).

999,208 is even, so we subtract 99 (999,109) and multiply by 3 (2,997,327 looping to 997327).

997,327 is odd, so we subtract 15 (997,312) and multiply by 2 (1,994,624 looping to 994,624), which we output as the final answer.
